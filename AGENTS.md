# Time Sheet Linux development

Before assessing, changing or reviewing this app, read `docs/AUTOMATION_ROADMAP.md`.
It is the user's accepted product source of truth: record personal and work
computer screen time automatically; Jev/TypeSafe should organise permitted
activity and ask the user only about meaningful exceptions.

For each change, update the roadmap's evidence ledger and relevant tests. Preserve
recorded evidence, explicit privacy choices, reversible draft allocations and
billing protections. Do not substitute extra manual screens for background
intelligence. Do not report aspirational scores or simulated accuracy as measured
results. Perform a fresh second-pass review after implementation; real provider,
hardware and accessibility acceptance must be recorded as unverified until tested.
